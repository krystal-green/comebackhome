An explosion that changed everything

23-year-old Christine Tavley has been searching for the truth behind the incident that destroyed her life five years ago. The explosion of 22nd March 1982 wiped her hometown, Totin, off the map. It was the beginning of the serial bomber known as Absinthe. Hiding behind the symbol of a green fairy, Absinthe comes alive with a broadcast. Two weeks later, another town vanishes just like Totin.

Christine's survival is fueled by the people she has left, the old memories, and the hope that she will one day get to the bottom of what Absinthe really wants. She visits the deserted town in search of answers which ultimately forces her to look closer than she expected. Getting closer to the truth feels freeing — until she overhears something she wasn't supposed to.

The truth that was supposed to be worth the loss becomes deadly, trapping her in ways she had never imagined.

About the Author

First of all, she is a wonderful person by nature and heart. This book along with her dream to become a writer started in 2016. And she was 13 at that time. Her writing was not good. Neither was her story. But ever since she was young, she has been creating stories. They always materialized in the real world in some ways, whether it was creating a 'movie' via her mother's phone with her toys as the cast or imagining scenarios where people were always in need of a savior (conveniently, She was always available to save people). Throughout her life, she has changed and so has her thinking. And that has contributed to her story evolving a lot since it first started.
